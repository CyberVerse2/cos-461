# Exam Quiz Design

## Scene
A student is revising in a quiet room with a laptop open and a phone nearby, switching between focused recall and quick review. The UI should feel clear in daylight and late evening without becoming a dark developer dashboard.

## Theme
Light, warm-tinted neutral surfaces with a restrained green accent for study progress and selection. Use OKLCH colors in CSS.

## Typography
System UI stack. Compact product hierarchy with readable question text and answer prose capped to comfortable line lengths.

## Components
- Fixed top bar with progress, mode switch, and reset.
- Main quiz panel with question, reveal, self-grade, and navigation.
- Review/sidebar list for direct question access and completion state.
- Mobile layout collapses into a single-column study flow with sticky progress.

## Interaction
No hidden gestures. Answers reveal inline. Self-grading stores locally. Keyboard shortcuts are supported but not required.
