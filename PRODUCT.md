# Exam Quiz

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
Exam Quiz turns a programming-language exam question set into a focused study tool. It helps a student practice long-form concepts, recall model answers, and decide which topics need another pass before the exam.

## Users
Students revising from supplied exam questions on programming languages, data structures, control structures, and execution models. They are likely studying on a laptop or phone, moving quickly between recall, answer reveal, and review.

## Principles
- The first screen is the study experience, not a landing page.
- Questions and answers come from the supplied PDF. Do not fabricate missing model answers.
- Reduce study friction: one obvious next action, quick reveal, clear progress, and useful review state.
- Treat this as a production tool: resilient local state, responsive layout, and no demo-only dead ends.

## Tone
Calm, direct, and study-focused. Avoid hype, mascot language, and vague motivation copy.
